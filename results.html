Google Ads

Lakeview Smile Studio — Chicago, IL

Full-service general practice with declining ad performance over 12 months
172%
More conversions
25%
Lower cost/lead
10%
Conv. rate lift

What we found

  • Broad match keywords pulling in low-intent traffic — dental schools, job seekers, free clinics
  • No negative keyword list — budget bleeding on irrelevant searches for over a year
  • Zero conversion tracking — the practice had no idea which ads actually produced patients
  • Generic ad copy that didn't differentiate from competitors

What we did

  • Full account audit — paused wasteful campaigns, restructured from scratch
  • Replaced broad match with phrase and exact match keywords targeting real patients
  • Built a 400+ term negative keyword list to block irrelevant traffic
  • Installed call tracking and form attribution — every lead tied to its source
  • Wrote patient-focused ad copy and A/B tested against the old versions
  • Tightened geo-targeting to a 10-mile radius around the practice

"Fewer junk calls, more real patients walking through the door. We could finally see what our ad spend was actually doing."

— Dr. Amanda Park, Owner

14/16

What we found

  • Still spending on newspaper ads with no way to measure ROI
  • Google Ads reaching 900,000 people at 1.29% CTR — wildly too broad
  • All services lumped into one campaign — implants, cleanings, emergencies competing for the same budget
  • Every ad pointed to the homepage — no dedicated landing pages

What we did

  • Built separate campaigns per service: implants, braces, emergency, cosmetic, new patients
  • Created dedicated landing pages with appointment forms for each service
  • Tightened geo-targeting to a 4km radius — impressions dropped from 900K to 5K, but every one was relevant
  • Researched and targeted high-converting specific terms instead of generic keywords
  • Cost per new patient dropped to $70 — well below the $150–300 industry average
