We believe no patient call should go unanswered

DentAI was built by a team that saw how much revenue dental practices were losing to missed calls, voicemail, and slow follow-up — and decided to fix it.

The problem we saw

Dental practices spend thousands on ads to get the phone to ring. But when it does — during lunch, after hours, on busy mornings — 30–40% of those calls go straight to voicemail.

That's not a staffing problem. It's a structural one. No front desk can answer every call, every time. But patients don't leave voicemails — they call the next practice.

What we built

DentAI is an AI receptionist purpose-built for dental practices. It answers every call in under a second, books appointments into your real calendar, and follows up with patients automatically.

We pair it with review automation and reactivation campaigns — so you're not just answering calls, you're growing the practice. One AI system, no extra staff, live in under a week.
