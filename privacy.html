Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

Overview

DentAI ("we," "us," or "our") provides AI-powered receptionist and practice growth services for dental practices. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our website and services.

We are committed to handling data responsibly. DentAI is built on HIPAA-aware infrastructure and follows privacy-first principles in everything we build.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Contact information (name, email, phone number) when you book a demo or sign up
  • Practice information (practice name, address, services offered, hours, insurance accepted)
  • Billing information processed through our secure payment provider
  • Communications you send to us (emails, support requests, feedback)

Information collected through our services:

  • Call recordings and transcripts handled by the AI receptionist
  • Appointment booking data
  • Review request and response activity
  • Service usage and performance metrics

Website analytics:

  • Standard web analytics (page views, referral source, device type) collected through privacy-respecting tools
  • We do not use invasive tracking, sell data to advertisers, or build behavioral profiles

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our AI receptionist and practice growth services
  • To configure the AI to accurately represent your practice
  • To communicate with you about your account, service updates, and support
  • To generate performance reports and analytics for your dashboard
  • To comply with legal obligations

HIPAA Awareness

DentAI is built on HIPAA-aware infrastructure. We take the following measures:

  • We do not store Protected Health Information (PHI) beyond what is necessary for service delivery
  • Call recordings and transcripts are processed with privacy-first protocols
  • All data transmission uses industry-standard encryption (TLS 1.2+)
  • Access to practice data is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • We will enter into a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with practices upon request

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted third parties that help us deliver our services (cloud hosting, payment processing, communication tools), bound by confidentiality agreements
  • Legal compliance: When required by law, subpoena, or legal process
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to affected users

Data Ownership & Portability

Your data belongs to you. Specifically:

  • All call recordings, transcripts, and booking logs generated through your DentAI service are your property
  • You can request a full export of your data at any time
  • Upon cancellation, we will provide your data and delete our copies within 30 days unless legally required to retain them

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption at rest and in transit, access controls, regular security assessments, and secure development practices.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@dentai.co.

Cookies

Our website uses only essential cookies required for basic functionality (session management, security). We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify active customers of material changes via email. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
